## Counter and interval vocabulary

- **Encoded bytes:** the exact framed/packed stream size produced by a codec or the 2:4 compressor (byte effect).
- **Payload DMA cycles:** `ceil(bytes / (dma_bus_width_bits/8))` — a deterministic analytical estimate, not elapsed time.
- **Decode cycles:** configured decoder-width ratios (`decoder_elements_per_cycle`, `rle_runs_per_cycle`, `bitmap_elements_per_cycle`, `sparsity_decoder_groups_per_cycle`) applied to element or metadata counts.
- **Total cycles:** serial sum or overlap max under the named `decoder_overlap_dma` flag; both are analytical, and neither is a queue-aware or calibrated quantity.
- **MAC counts:** dense `M*N*K` vs sparse `M*N*K/2`; useful-work counts, not cycle guarantees.
